# Sustain: requirements and applicability review

Reviewed **September 25, 2026** against the current repository and the official sources linked below. This is an engineering assessment and a list of decisions for qualified review, not a legal opinion, compliance certification or App Review guarantee. Source pages can change; recheck before submission.

**Planning assumption: US-first, direct-to-consumer wellness app.** This is not an approved distribution scope. Operator identity/location, user territories, minimum age, monetization and support contacts remain unknown. The developer's location and the database region do not establish jurisdiction. Public release differs from the original personal-use project.

“Applicable” identifies a current platform requirement or engineering obligation for the proposed release. “Conditional” means geography, business role or product choices determine scope. “Unresolved” means evidence or an owner decision is missing. “Not applicable” refers only to today's observed feature set.

The rows are a launch triage, not a claim that all US or international laws have been enumerated. Add jurisdictions only after completing their assessment. The proposed technical architecture can support later expansion, but receiving health data from additional markets is an operational and legal change as well as a scaling question.
